Inverse-design challenge

Ten force curves measured on this rig; their patterns are secret. Find a pattern that reproduces one, the rig tests it, and your score goes on the leaderboard.

Targets and leaderboard

Each target below is a force curve measured on this rig. Its pattern is secret and not in the data set. Find a pattern that reproduces the curve: the rig tests your entry and scores it by the RMS difference between the two 200-point curves, in newtons (lower is better). The difference in displacement at 20 N is shown alongside.
