Inverse Design Challenge

Here are ten force curves measured on this rig. The patterns that produced them are secret. Your task is to find a pattern that reproduces one of these curves. You can design it in the editor or with your own code. The rig then tests your pattern and your score goes on the leaderboard.

Targets and leaderboard

Each target below is a measured force curve whose pattern is secret and not in the data set. When you submit an entry, the rig tests it and scores it by how closely its curve matches the target. The score is the root-mean-square (RMS) difference between the two curves in newtons. The difference in displacement at 20 N is shown alongside.

Work Offline: Download the Targets, Upload Your Entries

For your own algorithms: download all ten target curves, design patterns on your computer, then upload them as a zip. Each pattern is tested on the rig and scored against its target, just like an entry made in the editor.

Step 1

Download the Target Curves

A zip with the ten targets in the same format as the data set:

  • targets_dataData.npy: 10 × 200 force curves, stored as 2F/20 N − 1
  • targets_maxData.npy: displacement at 20 N, mm
  • targets.csv: target name, displacement at 20 N, buckling events
  • targets_curves.csv: the same curves as displacement (mm) and force (N)
  • README.txt: formats, scoring and how to submit

Step 2

Upload Your Entries

One .zip with two text files, at most one pattern per target (up to 10):

patterns (or patterns.txt)
One pattern per line: 105 digits of 0 and 1, bottom row first, 1 = insert.
targets (or targets.txt)
One target per line (T1 … T10), in the same order as patterns.
